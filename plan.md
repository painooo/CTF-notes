# How to organize notes & scripts?
- Notes & scripts are stored in seperate directories:
    - Scripts/ -> A random collection of scripts
    - Notes/ -> Notes :p
        - `source`/ -> Seperated according to source of info
            - Seperated further according to purpose of info (subjective)

# How to take notes?
1. Draft together information/observations from the source (don't bother w/ making it prety)
2. Organize & revise that draft (where you'll make it pretty)
3. Seperate information based on purpose (*optional*)
